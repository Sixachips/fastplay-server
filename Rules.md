# Domino rules

## Equipment & Setup
* **The Set**: Standard partnership dominoes uses a **Double-Six set of 28 tiles**, ranging from 0–0 to 6–6.
* **Seating & Teams**: The game is played strictly by **4 players in 2 fixed partnerships**. Teammates sit directly **opposite each other** at the table.
* **The Deal**: All **28 tiles are dealt** (7 tiles to each player), meaning there is no draw pile or boneyard.

---

## Opening the Game & Turn Rotation
* **First Hand**: The first hand of a session is opened by the player holding the **Double-Six [6–6]** tile, who must place it down to start the layout.
* **Subsequent Hands**: In subsequent rounds, the turn to open rotates counter-clockwise (or goes to the winner of the previous round), and the leading player may open with **any tile** of their choice.
* **Direction of Play**: Play moves in a **counter-clockwise direction** around the table.

---

## Matching & Turn Rules
* **Connecting Tiles**: Players take turns adding a single tile to either open end of the continuous chain, matching the touching numbers.
* **Placing Doubles**: Double tiles are played **crosswise** (at a 90-degree angle to the line). Doubles do not act as spinners, so the layout always maintains exactly **two open ends**.
* **Mandatory Play & Passing**: Voluntary passing is prohibited—if a player holds a legal tile, they **must play it**. If a player has no matching tile, they must **pass** their turn (typically by knocking on the table).

---

## Ending a Hand & Blocked Games
A hand ends when:
1. **Domino (Playing Out)**: A player plays their final tile, winning the hand for their partnership.
2. **Blocked Game (*Trancado*)**: No player holds a matching tile, and all four players pass in succession.

### Determining the Winner of a Blocked Game:
* Under **Standard / Latin rules**, the team with the **lowest combined pip total** in their remaining hands wins the round.
* Under **Jamaican / Caribbean rules**, the team holding the **single player with the fewest remaining pips** wins the round.
* If remaining pip counts are tied between opposing teams, the round ends in a **tie** and no points are awarded.

---

### Scoring Systems
* **Standard Cumulative Scoring (100 Points)**: The winning team earns points equal to the total pips remaining in the **losing team's hand** (or all unplayed tiles). The first team to reach a target of **100 points** wins the match.
* **Jamaican / Caribbean Scoring (6-Love)**: Each hand win awards **1 point**. A team must win **6 consecutive points** to win the match. If the opposing team wins a hand, the leading team's score resets back to 0.
